TITLE (max 30)
Audiation — hear inwardly

SHORT (max 80)
Hear music inwardly. Eleven exercises, from about age 7.

FULL (max 4000)
Audiation is an app for hearing music inwardly. You listen, it goes silent, and you hold the sound in your head. Then you check: slide, tap, sing or play. Singing or an instrument is a check, not the aim.

Names (Do or 1) come after hearing — except in Find a note. This is not a Music Learning Theory course and not a substitute for a teacher. Practice from about age 7.

The path has eleven steps, easy to hard. New users can set every exercise to the simplest options at once: one octave, cue tone on, short lines.

• Hold a tone — one tone from C major, silence, hold it inwardly
• Find a note — you see a letter, then slide to it
• Hold an interval — two tones, keep the distance
• Melody — a short line, tap heights, no staff
• Reverse — tap the line backwards
• Hold a chord — one harmony in the silence
• Harmony — slide a second tone in until it fits
• Extensions — 9, 11 or 13 on a chord
• Progressions — degrees of the roots, in C
• Rhythm — listen and tap back, or tap one in yourself
• Tempo — metronome, tap a note value, keep the pulse inwardly

Then you can open each exercise up: more octaves, cue off, longer lines, inversions. Advanced: sound character (plain first, later combo, band or orchestra).

Tools: piano (C major) and a tuning check at A440. Languages: Dutch, English, German, French, Spanish.

The microphone is optional and stays on the device: the app listens to your voice or instrument to check the pitch. No account needed.
